# Sales Analytics & Profitability Dashboard | Microsoft Excel

An interactive Excel dashboard for analyzing sales performance, revenue trends, profitability, and regional performance through data visualization and KPI reporting.

## Overview
This project demonstrates the use of Microsoft Excel to transform structured sales data into actionable business insights. The dashboard consolidates key performance indicators, monthly sales trends, country-level comparisons, sales channel contributions, and regional analysis into a single interactive reporting interface.

The workbook combines sales data, calculated financial metrics, Pivot Tables, charts, and interactive filters to support business performance monitoring and data-driven decision-making.

## Business Objectives
- Monitor overall revenue and profitability.
- Analyze monthly revenue and gross profit trends.
- Compare performance across countries and regions.
- Evaluate revenue contributions by sales channel.
- Explore product category performance.
- Present key business metrics in an interactive dashboard.

## Dashboard Features
- **Executive Summary:** KPI cards for revenue, gross profit, gross margin, units sold, sales records, and cost of goods sold.
- **Monthly Trend Analysis:** Visual comparison of revenue and gross profit over time.
- **Country Analysis:** Comparison of revenue across countries and identification of leading markets.
- **Sales Channel Analysis:** Evaluation of revenue contributions from online, retail, wholesale, and distributor channels.
- **Regional Analysis:** Comparison of sales performance across European regions.
- **Product Category Analysis:** Exploration of Camping, Cycling, Fitness, Hiking, and Travel categories.
- **Interactive Filters:** Dashboard filtering by product category, country, and quarter.
- **Geographic Visualization:** Map-based presentation of country-level revenue.

## Dataset
The workbook contains 2,707 sales records and 20 columns, with order dates ranging from January 1 to December 30, 2026.

The dataset includes:
- Order identifiers and dates
- Year, quarter, and month
- Region and country
- Sales channel and customer type
- Product category and product name
- Units sold, unit price, and discount percentage
- Unit cost and cost of goods sold
- Revenue, gross profit, and gross margin

## How to Use
1. Download the Excel workbook from this repository.
2. Open it in Microsoft Excel.
3. Navigate to the `Dashboard` worksheet.
4. Review the KPI cards and visualizations.
5. Use the available filters to explore different data segments.
6. Examine the `Pivot Tables` worksheet for summarized results.
7. Review the `Data` worksheet to understand the underlying records.

Desktop Microsoft Excel is recommended for the best compatibility with interactive filters and visualizations.
